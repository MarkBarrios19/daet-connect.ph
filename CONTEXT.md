# CONNECT-DAET

A tourism site for Daet, Camarines Norte: visitors find places and tours run by
verified local operators, plan trips with an AI assistant, and book.

## Language

### People

**Visitor**:
Anyone using the site to plan or take a trip to Daet, signed in or not.
_Avoid_: User, customer, guest (guest is a person on a booked tour)

**Group organiser**:
A visitor planning and booking for a group of about 6 to 20 people (barkada,
family, office outing).
_Avoid_: Group leader, admin

**Operator**:
A local business, verified by the Daet Tourism Office, that runs tours.
_Avoid_: Vendor, provider, agency

### The assistant

**Assistant**:
The AI that answers visitors' questions, reached from the floating chat button
on every page. It is the visitor's main way in.
_Avoid_: Chatbot, bot, widget, AI guide

**Planner**:
The full-page view where a plan is drafted and changed beside the chat. The
assistant hands plan requests over to it.
_Avoid_: Itinerary builder, trip builder

**Basic mode**:
The assistant answering from fixed rules and listings: when the AI is
unavailable, after a visitor's daily allowance of AI answers, or after 3
messages a day from a visitor who is not signed in.
_Avoid_: Offline mode, fallback mode

### Trips

**Plan**:
A day-by-day list of stops drafted for a visitor's dates and group, not yet saved.
_Avoid_: Itinerary (in visitor-facing text), schedule

**Sample plan**:
A plan drafted without dates, to show what a trip could look like; it gets dates
before it can be saved or booked.
_Avoid_: Draft, example itinerary

**Stop**:
One place visited in a plan, with a time and a cost.
_Avoid_: Activity, item, point

**Saved trip**:
A plan the visitor has kept in their account.
_Avoid_: Saved itinerary, my trip

**Tour**:
A product an operator sells, with a price and dates, that can be booked.
_Avoid_: Package (in visitor-facing text), trip (when meaning the product)

**Bookable**:
Said of a stop that a listed tour passes through, so it can be booked.

**Booking**:
A visitor's request for a tour on a date for a number of guests, confirmed by
the operator and then paid.
_Avoid_: Reservation, order

**Call-off**:
An operator cancelling a booked tour for weather or safety; the visitor picks a
new date or gets a full refund.
_Avoid_: Cancellation (that is the visitor's own choice)

**Trip day**:
A day on which the visitor is in Daet doing their saved trip or booked tour.
